How we use your coaching information

Coach: agree this wording with your Club Welfare Officer and replace anything in italics before you send links out.

Who we are

This questionnaire is run by your coach at [club name]. Contact: [coach or Club Welfare Officer email].

What we collect

Your name (juniors: first name and initial), squad, age group, your cricket experience and goals, your own skill ratings, and any injuries or medical conditions you choose to tell us about. For juniors we also record a parent or guardian's name and consent.

Why

To plan coaching around you, set development goals with you and track your progress. We rely on your consent (or your parent or guardian's consent for juniors). Health information is only used to coach you safely.

Who sees it

Only your coach. It isn't published, sold or shared with anyone else. It is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) in [region, e.g. London].

How long we keep it

[For example: while you are coached by us, and deleted within 12 months after you stop.]

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, or withdraw consent, by contacting [email].
